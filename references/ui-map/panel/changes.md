# The workspace changes card

Above the conversation (on the list, the general conversation's): "Workspace changes · N", and how many of them the
work shown draws on. Collapsed until pressed; opened by itself when Tailor documents or Fill form waits on a change.
Not there when nothing is out of date.

**Marker:** `css=details.ez-panel-changes`

| Control | When | Selector | Does | Leads to |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Workspace changes | | `css=details.ez-panel-changes > summary` | Opens or closes the list | [Changes list](changes-list.md), [Changes card](changes.md) |
