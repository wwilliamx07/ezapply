# Application tracker: progress and outcomes

**What.** "What's in flight?" in the panel's list of applications: each one's deadline, date submitted, what the
employer did after, and the next action.

**Why.** The panel's list already groups applications by what they wait on and how far they've got, but shows no
deadline or submission date, and nothing records what happened after submitting.

**Plan.**
- An optional outcome in the posting's record, beside its progress, set from the posting's menu in the panel (as
  Mark submitted is) or by telling the agent.
- The panel's rows show deadline, submitted date and outcome; open applications sort by deadline.
- The agent's postings list gains the same fields and filters.
